# Glossary

**EFMW-style recursive coherence monitor:** recursively accumulates signed prediction residuals and scores the magnitude of the accumulated state.

**Baseline residual EWMA:** exponentially weighted moving average of absolute one-step residuals.

**Frozen experiment:** equations, seeds, thresholds, endpoints, and analysis rules are immutable before confirmation.

**Paired lead-time gain:** EFMW warning lead minus baseline warning lead on the same trial.

**Raw FPR:** fraction of pre-onset samples whose score exceeds the frozen threshold.
